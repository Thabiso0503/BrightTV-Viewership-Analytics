# 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of **BrightTV's user behaviour and viewing patterns** to support the Customer Value Management (CVM) team in achieving its strategic objective of growing the platform's subscription base.

Using SQL and data analytics techniques, the project uncovers viewing trends, audience engagement patterns, and content consumption behaviour to generate actionable business recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

BrightTV aims to:

- Increase its subscription base
- Improve user engagement
- Enhance customer retention
- Support data-driven business decisions

This analysis helps identify:

- Viewer behaviour patterns
- Content consumption trends
- Growth opportunities
- Customer engagement strategies

# 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 User & Viewing Trends

- Most users belong to the **18–35** age group.
- Highest viewing activity occurs during:
  - Evenings (18:00–22:00)
  - Weekends

**Insight:** BrightTV primarily attracts a young, entertainment-focused audience.

---

### 📈 Consumption Drivers

Major factors influencing content consumption include:

- Free time after work or school
- Relevant and entertaining content
- Mobile accessibility
- Trending shows and popular releases

**Insight:** Users prefer engaging, relatable, and easily accessible content.

---

### 📉 Low Engagement Patterns

Lower platform activity occurs during:

- Monday to Wednesday
- Morning hours

**Insight:** Users are less likely to engage during busy work or school periods.

---

### 🎥 Content Strategy Opportunities

Potential opportunities to improve engagement include:

- Short-form content
- Daily episodes
- Mini-series
- Interactive features
- Push notifications

# 💡 Business Recommendations

## 📺 Content Strategy

- Invest in youth-oriented programming
- Produce trending and locally relevant content
- Expand entertainment offerings

## 📈 Engagement Strategy

- Implement personalized recommendations
- Send notifications during peak viewing periods
- Improve content discovery

## 📅 Weekday Activation

- Launch weekday-exclusive content
- Introduce gamification
- Reward consistent viewers

## 📢 Marketing Strategy

- Target younger audiences through social media
- Collaborate with influencers
- Promote trending content

## 🔁 Retention Strategy

- Improve user experience
- Offer subscription bundles
- Introduce loyalty rewards
- Reduce customer churn through personalized engagement
